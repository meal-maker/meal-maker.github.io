## [Google AI Shakeup: Hassabis Chair, Dean & Ghemawat Exit](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google announced that Demis Hassabis will transition from CEO of Google DeepMind to its Chair, while longtime engineers Jeff Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat are leaving to found an independent AI startup. These changes represent a significant reshaping of Google’s AI leadership, raising concerns about a talent exodus and the company’s ability to maintain its edge in the AI race. Hassabis’s new role as Chair likely reduces his day-to-day involvement, while Jeff Dean, Google’s chief scientist, and Sanjay Ghemawat, a Senior Fellow, leave after a combined 50+ years at the company. The stock dropped 5% on the news.

**Background**: Demis Hassabis co-founded DeepMind and led it after Google’s acquisition, contributing to breakthroughs like AlphaGo. Jeff Dean is a legendary engineer behind many Google systems, including TensorFlow and MapReduce. Sanjay Ghemawat is also a key figure in Google’s infrastructure. Their departures mark the end of an era.

**Discussion**: The community views the departures as a significant talent drain, with one observer noting that Google has lost many prominent AI researchers in recent months without notable new hires. ## [Cloudflare OS: Open Platform for AI Agents, Apps, and Work](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-os/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Cloudflare has launched Cloudflare OS, an open-source platform built on Cloudflare Workers that enables developers to create AI agents and applications, originally developed for internal use and now publicly available. This platform could simplify the development of AI-powered agents by leveraging Cloudflare’s global edge network, but it also raises concerns about vendor lock-in and the overuse of the term 'OS' for branding. Cloudflare OS is a chatbot with connectors, reminiscent of the defunct Sandstorm.io project, and runs on the serverless Workers platform, with the source code available on GitHub.

hackernews · speckx · Aug 5, 13:58 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49182996)

**Background**: Cloudflare Workers is a serverless computing service that lets developers run code at the edge, close to users, without managing servers. AI agents are autonomous programs that use large language models to perform tasks, and platforms like Cloudflare OS aim to standardize their development and deployment.
