## [Google Discloses $94.1B Stake in SpaceX, a 6% Holding](https://www.wsj.com/tech/google-discloses-94-1-billion-in-spacex-stock-marking-6-stake-91655d7c) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Alphabet disclosed that its stake in SpaceX is now valued at $94.1 billion, representing a 6% ownership, up from an original investment of about $900 million. This massive return highlights the immense value growth of private space companies and validates Alphabet's strategic investment in high-growth technology sectors. It underscores the potential for corporate venture capital to yield outsized returns. The initial investment was part of a ~$1 billion funding round led by Google and Fidelity in 2015, which valued SpaceX at about $10–12 billion. The current valuation implies a roughly 100x return, and the 6% stake exceeds the typical 5% IPO share float, indicating SpaceX remains tightly held.

**Background**: SpaceX, founded by Elon Musk, is a private aerospace manufacturer and space transportation company. Alphabet, Google’s parent company, has a history of early investments in transformative tech firms, such as Android, YouTube, and DeepMind. The 2015 investment came as SpaceX was developing reusable rockets and planning its Starlink satellite internet constellation, which have since driven its valuation to over $150 billion in private markets.
