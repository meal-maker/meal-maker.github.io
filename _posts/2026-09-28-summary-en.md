Legacy retail colocation facilities are being rapidly retrofitted into AI clusters using high-density electrical and liquid cooling upgrades, creating the world&\#x27;s second-largest physical compute base after North America. ByteDance controls nearly 20% of China&\#x27;s delivered capacity and achieved a 100MW delivery milestone at a core site within 12 months. telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 27, 08:36

**「Background」** Data center capacity is typically measured in gigawatts of commissioned power load rather than server count; EMEA refers to Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, while Asia-Pacific in this comparison excludes China. **「Impact」** China&\#x27;s cloud vendors now control a much larger AI-capable infrastructure base, but the first-ever simultaneous negative free cash flow at Alibaba, Tencent, and Baidu indicates the buildout is consuming cash faster than operations generate it.

### [Boeing 737 MAX Software Defect May Disable Autopilot During Landing](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/world/story20260927-9742415) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Boeing has disclosed a previously unknown 737 MAX software defect that can cause the autopilot to fail during landing. The fault originates in a cockpit software update and can be triggered after a crew performs a go-around and changes route. The U.S. Federal Aviation Administration is investigating, and Southwest Airlines and United Airlines have asked Boeing not to deliver new aircraft equipped with the software. Boeing notified all 737 operators last month and is developing a permanent update, but it is unclear how many in-service aircraft carry the software.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 27, 05:53

**「Background」** Boeing&\#x27;s 737 MAX is a narrow-body airliner whose flight automation includes autopilot and automated flight guidance for navigation and landing. A &\#x27;go-around&\#x27; is an aborted landing procedure in which pilots apply takeoff thrust and climb away; during such maneuvers, the autopilot may be disconnected and later re-engaged. The U.S. Federal Aviation Administration investigates reported software anomalies to assess airworthiness and determine whether operational restrictions or corrective software updates are required.
