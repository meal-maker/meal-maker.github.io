### [Passkeys Are a Trap](https://nonfunctional.substack.com/p/passkeys-are-a-trap-do-not-use-them) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A Substack essay titled “Passkeys are a trap” argues that passkeys can lead to platform lock-in and other problems, prompting significant technical discussion. Community comments note that attestation can only be required at registration, browsers show permission prompts, and portability remains a major user concern. One commenter points out the author’s footnote concedes passkeys are domain-bound and resistant to man-in-the-middle attacks, while others discuss legitimate uses of remote attestation.

hackernews · laurex · Oct 11, 21:59 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50048312)

**「Background」** Passkeys are passwordless credentials based on public key cryptography, tied to a user account and a specific website or application; they are implemented through the FIDO2/WebAuthn standards and can be synced across devices by platform providers like Apple, Google, and Microsoft. Attestation is an optional WebAuthn mechanism that verifies the make and model of a passkey authenticator during registration, and it can be used by relying parties to restrict which passkeys are accepted. This article argues that such platform-dependent syncing and attestation create lock-in risks, prompting debate over passkey portability and user control.

**「Community Discussion」** Comments are split: some criticize passkeys for portability and lock-in concerns, while others clarify that attestation is only enforceable during registration and note the article&\#x27;s own footnote acknowledges domain-bound MITM resistance and legitimate attestation uses.

### [Sliced Wasserstein Embeddings and HNSW for Color-Scheme Search over 48k Paintings](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1x39uu6/sliced_wasserstein_embeddings_hnsw_for/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Palette Atlas introduces sliced Wasserstein embeddings combined with a from-scratch HNSW index to search 48,695 public-domain paintings by color scheme. Each painting is reduced to 128 equal-weight colors, and colors in OKLab are projected onto 8 Fibonacci-hemisphere directions, sorted, and averaged into 16 quantiles; L1 distance then approximates the 1D Wasserstein distance averaged over directions and lower-bounds exact Earth Mover&\#x27;s Distance. On 240 queries, the 8x16 L1 embedding achieves 78% overlap with exact top-10 EMD results and only 1.8% farther top-10 distance, versus 69%/3.4% for L2, 45%/13.5% for Hellinger soft histograms, and 3%/161% for mean color. The HNSW index with M=16 and efConstruction=200 reaches recall@10 of 1.000 at ef=32 in 0.25 ms, compared with 14 ms for brute force. The implementation modifies the top HNSW layer to use 8 k-means landmarks rather than random nodes, retaining comparable search cost and making the top layer represent typical color families.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Potential-Barber8658 · Oct 11, 14:39

**「Background」** Earth Mover&\#x27;s Distance \(Wasserstein-1\) measures the minimal cost to transform one color distribution into another, which is a natural but expensive metric for 128-color palettes. Sliced Wasserstein embeddings reduce high-dimensional EMD by projecting points onto random directions and using one-dimensional Wasserstein distances. HNSW is a graph-based approximate nearest-neighbor index that provides fast querying with a small loss of recall.
