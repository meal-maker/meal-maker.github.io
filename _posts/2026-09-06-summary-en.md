### [Declarative Attention Lets Language Models Reduce KV Cache Scanning](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1w7sgf3/language_models_can_control_their_own_attention_r/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The paper introduces Declarative Attention \(DA\), a protocol where language models declare whether to attend globally, focus on a specific region, or use only local context during chain-of-thought generation. The inference engine parses these declarations like tool calls and skips most of the KV cache read instead of scanning the full context. In zero-shot evaluation across 15 long-context tasks, DA on off-the-shelf Gemma-4-31B and Qwen-3.6-27B reduced total attended tokens during decoding by 52.0% and 31.1%, respectively, with modest accuracy drops of 1.27 and 2.75 percentage points. The accuracy drops shrink with model scale. The authors note that DA unlocks a new axis of sparse attention with potential for training-based improvements.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/eigenlaplace · Sep 5, 06:07

**「Background」** In transformer generation, attention over all previous tokens requires reading the entire key-value \(KV\) cache at each step, making long-context decoding expensive. Existing approaches pre-select relevant tokens using lightweight proxy scores, but this extrinsic scoring still costs O\(N\) per step. Declarative Attention instead relies on the model&\#x27;s own knowledge of which context regions are relevant, requesting an intrinsic declaration before generation.

**「Impact」** For deployments using Gemma-4-31B or Qwen-3.6-27B on long-context tasks, this intrinsic protocol could cut KV cache read costs during decoding by roughly half and a third, respectively, with modest accuracy loss that becomes smaller at larger model scale.
