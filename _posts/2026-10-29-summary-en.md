### [Apple&\#x27;s git still trusts compromised eMudhra certificates](https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/apple.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple&\#x27;s macOS still trusts certificates from the compromised eMudhra certificate authority, so system-provided utilities—including Apple-shipped git—continue to treat them as valid. Apple&\#x27;s recommended mitigation is manual editing of the certificate chain, but this change can be reverted by subsequent OS updates, reintroducing the issue. The affected trust store is the OS-level /etc/ssl, so the problem extends beyond git to other system utilities that rely on it.

hackernews · galadran · Oct 28, 11:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50271640)

**「Background」** eMudhra is a certificate authority whose certificates were compromised, which can allow malicious actors to impersonate legitimate services. Apple was notified of the eMudhra incident on 17 October, but as of 28 October, Apple-shipped developer tools including git, curl, and python3 still trusted the compromised certificates. Manual removal of the root certificates is possible, but macOS updates may restore them.

**「macOS command-line tools remain at risk」** Developers using Apple-shipped macOS command-line tools such as git and curl remain exposed to potential TLS interception because compromised eMudhra CA certificates are still present in the OS-level trust store; Apple&\#x27;s support page describes automatic blocking and optional manual steps, but those manual removals can be reverted by system updates, reintroducing the vulnerability.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters agree this is a serious trust-chain failure and note that Apple&\#x27;s guidance to manually patch the certificate chain is impractical because updates may undo the fix. Some point out that Homebrew-installed git and curl may not use the system store, but this remains unverified, and one commenter speculates the weak key generation was intentional despite no evidence.

### [Zero-Training Null-Space PCA Injection Boosts EmbeddingGemma-2 to Top MAEB](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xho7pr/r_taking_embeddinggemma2_577m_from_rank_26_to_1/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

By projecting PCA-reduced intermediate Conformer layers into the unused 256-dimensional null space of google/embeddinggemma-2&\#x27;s final projection matrix, a Reddit experiment lifts the model&\#x27;s MAEB audio benchmark macro score from 49.3% to 54.4% \(+5.1 points\) with no additional training, moving it from rank \#26 to \#1 on audio-only MAEB and \#16 to \#1 on full MAEB. The approach compresses Layer 2 and Layer 6 to 256 dimensions using PCA fitted on 1,400 clips from four external datasets and injects the result into directions orthogonal to all text vectors, leaving text embeddings bit-for-bit unchanged and text-to-audio retrieval within 0.05 points. Reported audio-to-audio gains include gender clustering from 0.05% to 63.1%, artist retrieval from 55.2% to 93.8%, emotion from 19.7% to 40.2%, and environmental sounds from 38.8% to 56.3%. Caveats include loss of Matryoshka truncation and drops on spoken-intent tasks such as SIBFLEURS from 57.2% to 21.3% and MInDS14 from 97.6% to 88.3%, though the author suggests training restricted to the null space could improve results further.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/JeanMichelRanu · Oct 28, 13:34

**「Background」** EmbeddingGemma-2 is a 577M text-aligned audio embedding model whose text backbone has hidden size 512 and whose terminal projection matrix W has shape 768 x 512 with no activation, so every text embedding lies in a 512-dimensional subspace and leaves 256 orthogonal directions unused. The MAEB benchmark on the MTEB leaderboard evaluates 30 audio tasks, including multilingual text-to-audio retrieval, unsupervised audio clustering, vocal emotion recognition, and environmental sound classification. Probing the model&\#x27;s 12 Conformer layers shows that intermediate layers preserve paralinguistic and acoustic attributes such as gender and emotion that final text-aligned embeddings largely discard.

**「Impact」** Developers working with text-aligned audio embedders can adopt this zero-training null-space PCA injection to recover discarded acoustic and paralinguistic features and improve MAEB audio-only rank from \#26 to \#1, but they should independently verify the self-reported results and account for the loss of Matryoshka truncation.

### [Generic Feedback Matches Self-Written Critiques in Hindsight Self-Distillation](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xhv8yp/how_much_does_the_hint_matter_a_generic_feedback/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A research announcement reports that in hindsight self-distillation for multi-step financial reasoning on GPT-OSS-20B, replacing the student&\#x27;s self-written critique with a fixed generic feedback line matches the full gain of about 3 points. The student identifies its first wrong step, a frozen copy conditions on the prefix plus the critique, and the student is distilled under reverse KL. Critiques from a 6x larger model do not materially improve performance. The method, Fin-SD, matches OPSD from full reference solutions while using 9.5% fewer tokens. This indicates that positional information, not critique content, drives the training gains.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/jhzyu · Oct 28, 17:52

**「Background」** Hindsight self-distillation trains a model on its own outputs after observing the outcome, typically using a frozen copy and reverse KL to transfer improved behavior back. Multi-step financial reasoning requires models to produce a sequence of reasoning steps, where identifying the first wrong step provides a conditioning signal for later tokens.

**「Impact」** For multi-step financial reasoning on GPT-OSS-20B, practitioners may replace self-written critiques with a fixed generic feedback line and retain the ~3 point gain, reducing the need for high-quality critique content. These results come from a single model and financial reasoning domain, so the limits in other settings remain to be tested.
