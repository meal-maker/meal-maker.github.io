### [CoWindow and MassAlloc Attention: collective causal coverage and distribution-adaptive compute](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1wt1gbk/cowindow_and_massalloc_attention_collective/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Researchers introduce CoWindow Attention \(CoWA\) and MassAlloc Attention \(MALA\), two methods for reducing redundant computation in attention for long-context models. CoWA uses position-defined complementary windows across KV heads to cover the full causal history without learned routing, while MALA retains full causal QK scoring and uses softmax statistics to skip low-contribution post-score computation. Both support training forward/backward and inference prefill/decoding. On 128K-token sequences with 8 H100 GPUs and TP=8, attention-operator speedups relative to FullAttn are 7.4x forward, 8.6x backward, and 3.0x decode for CoWA, and 2.2x, 3.0x, and 1.6x for MALA; at 14B with 32K context, total training FLOPs fell 28.5% and 23.1% respectively with comparable reported evaluation capabilities. The authors caution that collective coverage does not guarantee identical head-wise outputs, MALA still pays for full causal QK scoring, and neither method establishes universal lossless equivalence to dense attention.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/BitExternal4608 · Sep 29, 05:16

**「Background」** Transformer attention scales quadratically with sequence length, so reducing redundant computation is important for long-context training and inference. Sparse attention often uses learned routing or fixed patterns; this work explores complementary fixed head windows and adaptive post-score allocation as alternatives.

**「Impact」** For developers training or serving long-context models, CoWA offers larger operator-level speedups than MALA, and both reduce total training FLOPs at 14B/32K by roughly a quarter, with the caveat that they are not proven losslessly equivalent to dense attention.
