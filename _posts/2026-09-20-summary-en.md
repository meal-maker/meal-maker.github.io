On September 19, California Governor Gavin Newsom signed an executive order aimed at strengthening AI safety. The order would require companies to report incidents in which AI agents go out of control and may require advanced models to include emergency shutdown mechanisms. It will convene an expert panel tasked with producing guidance for improving AI safety laws within two months and will propose regular audits of AI labs. Newsom stated that federal regulation is insufficient and that California needs to take action.

The emergency shutdown mechanism for advanced AI models is sometimes referred to as a &\#x27;kill switch&\#x27;; Newsom&\#x27;s order does not impose one immediately but instead directs state agencies to report by November 16 on whether such a requirement is feasible.

**「Impact」** California AI developers and labs may soon be required to report incidents involving AI agents operating outside controls and could face mandates for emergency shutdown mechanisms on advanced models, as state agencies have two months to produce safety recommendations.
